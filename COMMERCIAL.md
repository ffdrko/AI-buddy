## What Counts as Commercial Use

You are using this software commercially if you are:

- Deploying it as a product or service that generates revenue
- Integrating it into a paid platform, SaaS, or subscription offering

- Reselling, white-labelling, or repackaging it in any form

Academic or personal study with no commercial application is not commercial use.
If you are unsure, contact us before proceeding.

## License Terms (Summary)

A commercial license includes the following mandatory terms:

### 1. Attribution

All public-facing materials — including but not limited to your product UI, website,
documentation, press releases, and app store listings — must include a visible credit:

> Built on technology developed by Fahim (github.com/ffdrko)

Attribution must not be hidden, greyed out, or placed in a location a typical user
would not see.

### 2. Revenue Share

A percentage of gross revenue directly attributable to features derived from this
software is owed to the copyright holder. The exact percentage is negotiated per
agreement, typically in the range of **5–15% of attributable gross revenue**,
payable quarterly.

"Attributable revenue" means revenue from any product, feature, or service that
would not exist or function without this codebase or a substantial derivative of it.

### 3. Reporting

Licensees must provide quarterly revenue reports covering the attributable product
or service, within 30 days of each quarter end.

### 4. Audit Right

The copyright holder reserves the right to request an independent audit of revenue
figures with 30 days' notice, no more than once per year.

### 5. No Sublicensing

A commercial license grants rights to the licensee only. You may not sublicense,
transfer, or assign your license to any third party without written consent.
